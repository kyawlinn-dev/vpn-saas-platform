# NovaNet MM Support & Contact

Where customers reach us when they need help, and what to expect.

## Facts (canonical values)

**Official support channels:**

- **Telegram Bot (primary):** [@novanetmmvpn_bot](https://t.me/novanetmmvpn_bot) — 24/7 self-serve for buy / key retrieval / server switch / balance check
- **Telegram Support (human):** [@novanetmmadmin](https://t.me/novanetmmadmin) — reachable via **👤 Admin / Support** button in the bot
- **Facebook Page:** for public announcements and cross-post visibility

**Self-serve capabilities (no human needed):**
- Claim 5 GB / 7-day free trial → automatic on `/start`
- Get / re-get your Outline key → `🔑 Outline Key ရယူရန်`
- Check remaining data → `📊 လက်ကျန်စစ်ရန်`
- Switch between Singapore and Japan servers → `🌐 Server ပြောင်းရန်` (paid users)
- Download the Outline app → `📥 Download Outline` (picks correct store per device)
- Read the how-to guide in Burmese → `📖 အသုံးပြုနည်း`

**When human support is needed:**
- Payment issues (wrong amount sent, missing screenshot, refund questions)
- Bugs the bot / Mini App can't resolve (key not connecting after switching)
- Account-specific questions the bot doesn't have visibility on
- Questions outside the bot's self-serve scope

**Support hours (Myanmar time):**
- Best response: business hours (roughly 9 AM – 10 PM MMT)
- Late-night messages (past midnight): expect reply the next morning
- No formal SLA — this is a small operator, honest expectations only

**What to include when contacting Admin/Support:**
- Your Telegram username (so admin can look up your account)
- The order ID or transaction time if it's payment-related
- Screenshot of the error / issue if visible
- Which server + which device you're using

## Content angles (for content agent)

- **"Support ကို ဘယ်လိုဆက်သွယ်ရမလဲ" quick reference post** — clean
  channel-list card showing bot + direct admin handle
- **"Admin မလို ကိုယ်တိုင်လုပ်လို့ရတဲ့ အလုပ်များ" post** — list of self-serve
  capabilities so customers know when to skip admin
- **"Message ပို့တဲ့အခါ ဘာတွေ ထည့်ပေးရမလဲ" tip post** — teach customers
  to include order ID + screenshot for faster resolution
- **"Real Myanmar team, real support" trust post** — differentiator vs
  faceless international VPN services that ignore Myanmar customers

## Do NOT claim

- ❌ Do NOT claim a specific SLA time ("reply within 15 minutes",
  "24/7 live chat") — we're a small team, don't overpromise
- ❌ Do NOT claim we have a call center or phone support — Telegram only
- ❌ Do NOT publish the support username in Facebook post images —
  including it in captions is fine (public info), but poster-embedded
  handles invite scraping/impersonation
- ❌ Do NOT claim we have Facebook Messenger support — we don't route
  DMs there; funnel everything through Telegram Bot / @novanetmmadmin
- ❌ Do NOT promise resolution timeframes for complex issues (refunds,
  bug investigations) — safe framing is "we'll look into it"
- ❌ Do NOT use "customer service" phrasing that implies large staff —
  "our small team" or "admin" is honest and matches the actual size
