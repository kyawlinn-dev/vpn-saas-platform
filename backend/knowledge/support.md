# NovaNet MM Support

## Workspace-specific contact

Use the support contact displayed by the customer's reseller bot/Mini App. support_username and payment_info are reseller-managed. A NovaNet brand example is not another reseller's official support or payment account.

Self-service currently covers account linking, explicit trial when eligible, app/key guidance, balance, initial purchase, one queued next purchase and eligible server switching. Telegram's commands menu and inline buttons are current navigation; old persistent-keyboard labels are compatibility-only.

## When to contact support

Payment amount/receipt/review questions, access failing after refreshing the correct client link, uncertain provisioning, wrong workspace/account, or discrepancies between screens need scoped record inspection. The backend does not implement online bank payment processing.

Share the order ID/time, selected app/protocol, server/location and relevant error screenshot. Keep secrets/access URLs private; never request service-role keys, bot tokens or Telegram init data from customers.

Support operating hours, refund policy, human response time and service guarantees are business decisions not established by this source audit. Do not publish a 24/7 human-support promise or invent an SLA.

Q_EN: Where do I ask about payment review?
A_EN: Contact the support shown in the same reseller workspace where you submitted the receipt.
