# NovaNet MM Platform and Brand

## Verified platform facts

NovaNet MM is a multi-tenant reseller platform selling Marzneshin-backed Shadowsocks and VLESS access through white-label Telegram bots/Mini Apps. The platform owner manages servers, catalogue, reseller enablement and admin-owned workspace settings. Each reseller has its own customer scope, brand, support and payment details.

Customer API and Mini App hosting use the DigitalOcean Droplet at api.novanetmm.com and app.novanetmm.com. Admin/reseller dashboards use Cloudflare Pages. These hosting facts do not establish company incorporation, ownership identities, operating history, privacy policy or human-support hours.

Outline is a supported Shadowsocks client family, not the current panel/server provider. Avoid describing the entire platform as a Google/Jigsaw-operated service. VLESS uses the supported subscription clients.

## Brand copy boundaries

- Name the brand the customer actually opened.
- Use that workspace's official contact/payment details.
- Do not invent affiliations, team/legal identity, operating dates, customer counts, guaranteed speed, privacy/no-logs policies or service commitments from software behavior.
- Historical marketing materials live outside this application repository. Owner-approved brand/legal policy remains a separate source; these technical facts do not replace it.

Q_EN: Why do different bots have different names and payment details?
A_EN: Resellers operate their own branded workspaces on the same platform. Use the workspace where your account and order were created.
